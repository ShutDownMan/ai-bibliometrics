# Development and rule-audit records

The lexical screening rules were developed against the broad v14 corpus. The
supporting exploratory audit documents currently remain in `NOTES/`:

- `NOTES/screening_audit_report.md`
- `NOTES/screening_audit_borderline.csv`

These documents justify rule development, not confirmatory human validation.
In manuscript language, describe them as rule-development audits. Do not call
them independent human review unless the individual decisions were made by
independent human raters and that provenance is recorded.
