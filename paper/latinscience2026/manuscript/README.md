# Manuscript direction

## The article can credibly claim now

1. A reproducible, screened map of 711 studies on AI in scholarly communication
   and research workflows.
2. A sharp post-2023 expansion of this specific literature.
3. A descriptive shift in the composition of generic versus named GenAI and
   workflow versus governance work.
4. A positive association between technological specificity and governance
   orientation after accounting for year.

## Claims to defer or soften

- Do not claim the axes are human-validated until the two-rater dataset is
  analysed.
- Do not claim a smooth linear temporal increase in either axis.
- Do not treat clusters as discovered topic structure.
- Do not make citations a headline result or a measure of quality.

## Recommended 8–10 page story

**Contribution:** a transparent bibliometric map plus interpretable
embedding-derived dimensions for a tightly defined scholarly-communication
corpus.

**Central result:** the post-2023 expansion is not just more papers; it combines
named-model work with a governance/integrity discourse, while workflow
automation remains an important parallel strand.

**Essential visuals:**

1. selection flow and final-corpus composition;
2. annual production and the two dimension distributions by cohort;
3. T–G scatter with a year-adjusted fitted relationship;
4. after ratings arrive, automatic-score versus human-score calibration.

Use citations only as a short exploratory robustness subsection, or omit them
if their age-adjusted model is not ready.
